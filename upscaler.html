How this works. Browsers don't expose true Lanczos resampling directly, so this tool approximates it by upscaling in small controlled steps (each ≤2×) with high-quality bicubic interpolation at every stage, which preserves noticeably more detail than one large jump. The optional sharpen pass runs a light unsharp mask afterward to restore perceived edge crispness.

Honest limits. This is interpolation, not AI reconstruction — it enlarges cleanly without adding blur or blockiness, but it can't invent detail that isn't in the source (that's what generative super-resolution models do, and is a different class of tool). For genuinely soft or low-res source images, expect a clean, larger image rather than new sharpness.
